Quiet mail rules

Mute mail you don't want to be interrupted by. Each rule matches incoming messages; for the mail it matches you can hide it from the message list, switch off the desktop notification, leave it out of the unread count, silence the sound, or any combination.

From and To match anywhere in the address, ignoring case: a whole address, a domain (@shop.com) or a fragment (noreply). Subject and Body are regular expressions, also ignoring case. The fields you fill in must all match; empty fields are ignored. Every rule that matches applies its ticks.

Subject and Body take JavaScript (ECMAScript) regular expressions, the same syntax a browser console uses. Enter the pattern only, without surrounding slashes or flags; matching is always case-insensitive, so TEST and test behave identically.

Examples: From "@newsletter.shop.com" with "No desktop notification" silences that whole sender domain. Subject "^\[Jira\]" with "Exclude from unread count" keeps ticket mail out of the badge. To "alias+shop@" with "No notification sound" mutes only mail sent to that alias. Body "unsubscribe" catches bulk mail whatever the sender.

Note on Body: sender, recipient and subject arrive with the message list, but the body does not, so as soon as one enabled rule has a Body pattern, every new message is downloaded and decrypted locally to test it. That costs one request and some processing per message. Leave Body empty unless you need it.

"Exclude from unread count" applies to the messages the extension has loaded (see "Max emails per account" under General). A matching message older than that window still counts towards the badge.

Data

Synced data includes your account email addresses and per-account display settings (never passwords, tokens or keys). It is stored in your browser vendor's sync infrastructure.

The export file contains your account email addresses and display settings in plain text (never passwords, tokens or keys). Treat it as personal data and delete it after transferring.

Outputs verbose logs to the browser console for troubleshooting. May include email addresses and account details.

Records recent sign-in and token-refresh activity locally (no passwords or tokens) to help diagnose session/expiry problems. It survives browser restarts, so it is still available after a problem occurs.

Stores your last email list locally (encrypted) so your messages and the configured popup size appear instantly when you start your browser, instead of waiting for the first check. Only message details are stored (sender, subject, time), never message text. Off by default.

Removes the locally cached email list (used to show your messages instantly on browser start). Your accounts and settings are not affected; messages reload on the next check.

Resets all settings to their default values. Your accounts will not be affected.
